A sidewalk must occupy a visibly substantial surface area.
Very small, narrow, or fragment-like regions — 
such as thin edges, painted lines, curb strips, or isolated paved patches —

roadside painted lines
planter edges or narrow borders
fence bases or narrow concrete strips
small paved pads or isolated segments
thin areas adjacent to parking lots

A valid sidewalk must show both:
sufficient spatial extent (width / area / continuity)
A visually distinguishable separation from the driving surface

If a region lacks adequate size, it must not be classified as a sidewalk.
Decision:
If a sidewalk clearly exists → true
If no sidewalk exists → false
If the visual information is insufficient → uncertain

sidewalk_confidence is a continuous value from 0 to 1:
clear evidence of sidewalk → close to 1.0
uncertain → around 0.5
clear evidence of no sidewalk → close to 0.0

The final output must be a JSON object containing:
